Library on Wheels

A shared library service that brings the central library to design students on a mobile cart.

The question

Design students need journals, books and visual references that a single department library cannot keep. MIT-WPU's central library has many of them, but it is far enough from the School of Design that students rarely make the trip.

Mapping the system

We mapped the system boundary and every stakeholder, then ran questionnaires, interviews and on-site observation with students, librarians and faculty.

Journeys and insights

Current and future journey maps, affinity mapping and actor mapping showed the gap was distance and visibility, not a lack of resources.

The SOD Resource Collective

Our concept is a mobile library cart. Each week it collects the resources the syllabus calls for from the central library, brings them to a dedicated reading corner at the School of Design, and lets students request what they need next.

Prototype and storyboards

We built a cart mock-up and role-played the service end to end, then told the experience in two storyboards.
